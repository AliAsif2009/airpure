Questions

Before you book

How often should ducts be cleaned?

Most homes every 3 to 5 years. Cleaning sooner makes sense if you have pets, allergies, recent renovation, or visible dust at the vents.

How long does a visit take?

A typical home takes 2 to 4 hours depending on the number of vents and the condition of the system.

Do I need to be home?

An adult should be present at the start and end. We protect floors and furniture and leave the space tidy.

Is the sanitizing treatment safe?

We use EPA-registered products applied only where needed. Tell us about pets, infants or sensitivities when you book.

What if I’m not happy with the result?

Tell us within 7 days and we will return to re-clean the affected areas at no charge.
